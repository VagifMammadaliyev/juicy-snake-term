# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

This is a small, friendly project. We want everyone to feel welcome regardless of experience level, background, or identity.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate in discussions, issues, and pull requests.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Assume good intent and focus on what is best for the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language of any kind.
- Personal or political attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and pull requests that violate this Code of Conduct, and may ban repeat or serious offenders.

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it to the maintainer at <vagifmammadaliyev@outlook.com>. All reports will be handled confidentially.
